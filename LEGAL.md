# Authorized-use policy

CPE Access Atlas is intended for equipment that you own or are explicitly
authorized to administer.

ISP-supplied equipment may be rented, loaned, remotely managed, or subject to
contractual restrictions. Ownership of an Internet subscription does not
automatically mean ownership of the modem or router. Obtain written permission
from the owner or operator when the equipment is not yours.

The project does not authorize:

- access to another subscriber's equipment;
- Internet-wide or local-network scanning;
- password guessing, credential stuffing, or use of leaked credentials;
- interception of another person's traffic or credentials;
- access to ISP management systems;
- disabling safeguards on equipment owned by an ISP without permission.

Laws, contracts, warranty terms, and ISP policies vary. Contributors and users
are responsible for determining which rules apply to them. The 0BSD license
grants permission to use the project code; it does not grant permission to
access a device or network.

This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any ISP,
ZTE, or another equipment manufacturer.
